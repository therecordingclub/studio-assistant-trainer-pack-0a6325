Quizzes & the Final Exam

Five pop-quiz sets from Week 1 through Month 2, then the Membership Specs Final Exam. Multiple choice where there is one right answer, write-in where the assistant has to explain. Every answer comes from the study library, mostly the Membership Guide.

1 · Rooms 2 · Membership & credits 3 · Fees & setup 4 · Hospitality & access 5 · The program Final exam Answer key

On screen: answer a section, press Check answers. Multiple choice is scored on the spot; written answers reveal the model answer, and you mark them yourself. Clear and retake reshuffles the choices, so it can be run as many times as it takes.

Pop quizzes: pulled at random through Months 1 and 2. Give them cold, no notes. Short and oral is fine.

Final exam: end of Month 2. Closed-book, on the trainer's screen or printed. One point per question, all parts right. The four scenarios are two points each: one for the numbers, one for the reasoning. 29 points, pass at 27 (90%), because an assistant quotes these numbers to members. Retake until passed.

Teach one rule above all: unsure of a number? Look it up first. Never quote a price you are guessing.
