Quizzes & the Final Exam

Five pop-quiz sets from Week 1 through Month 2, then the Membership Specs Final Exam. Every answer comes straight from the study library, mostly the Membership Guide. The answer key at the bottom is for the trainer. Keep it closed while an assistant is taking a quiz.

1 · Rooms 2 · Membership & credits 3 · Fees & setup 4 · Hospitality & access 5 · The program Final exam Answer key

Pop quizzes: pulled at random through Months 1 and 2. Give them cold, no notes. Short and oral is fine.

Final exam: end of Month 2. Written, closed-book. One point per question, all parts right. The four scenarios are two points each: one for the numbers, one for the reasoning. 29 points, pass at 27 (90%), because an assistant quotes these numbers to members. Retake until passed.

Teach one rule above all: unsure of a number? Look it up first. Never quote a price you are guessing.

Pop quiz 1Month 1

The rooms

Name the five rooms and know what each one is for.

  1. Name all five studio rooms.
  2. Which room is the "Main Stage," and what is its hourly rate and minimum booking?
  3. The Live Room booking includes two other spaces. Which two?
  4. What does the Recording Room rent for per hour, and what is it built around?
  5. Which room is purpose-built for live streaming a full band, and how many synced cameras does it have?
  6. How many people does the Podcast Room seat, how many cameras does it have, and what does it cost per hour?
  7. A member wants to book the whole club for an event. What do you do, and what do you never do?
  8. True or false: all rooms are set up "plug-and-play" by default.
Pop quiz 2Month 1 to 2

Membership & credits

The tiers, the credits, and how credit actually works.

  1. Name the three membership tiers and their monthly cost.
  2. How much monthly studio credit does each tier get?
  3. What is the simple relationship between a tier's monthly cost and its credit?
  4. How long does unused studio credit roll over?
  5. What can studio credit be used for, and name one thing it cannot be used for.
  6. A member buys extra studio hours beyond their credit. What is the "double credit" benefit?
  7. Which tier includes 24-hour access, and on which days?
  8. True or false: studio credit can be spent on an engineer.
Pop quiz 3Month 2

Fees & setup

The numbers a member will ask you about at the desk.

  1. What is the Set Up / Breakdown fee for the Live Room? For the Podcast Room?
  2. A member changed the routing and left the room off-standard. What reset fee applies in the Live Room versus the other rooms?
  3. What is the Equipment Relocation fee for one piece of gear? For two or more?
  4. How many participants can a member bring to a session before they need studio approval, and what does each extra one cost after that?
  5. What is the guest day fee?
  6. Name the three house engineer roles and their starting hourly rates.
  7. When is a member required to have an engineer?
  8. What is in the TRC Full Production Package, what does it cost, and which room is it exclusive to?
  9. Half-day and full-day buyouts: how many hours, starting at what, and what is billed separately?

Hospitality & access

The vibe, the hours, and how members move through the space.

  1. When do assistants open, when do doors open to members, when do we close Tuesday to Saturday and on Sunday, and which day are we closed?
  2. Name the three pillars of the club.
  3. What is the club's full name, and who is the founder?
  4. When can members use the lounge, social, and wellness areas?
